Links

The following resources provide useful information about Island Pond, New Hampshire lakes, and environmental programs relevant to our community.

Water Quality & Conservation

  • NH Department of Environmental Services (DES)

    State agency overseeing water quality, the VLAP program, and exotic species management.

  • Volunteer Lake Assessment Program (VLAP)

    NH DES program that trains and coordinates volunteers to monitor lake water quality.

  • DES Exotic Species Program

    Information on identifying, controlling, and managing invasive aquatic plants in NH.

NH Lakes Association

  • NH Lakes Association (NHLA)

    Statewide organization dedicated to the protection and enjoyment of New Hampshire's lakes.

  • Lake Host Program

    NHLA program that stations lake hosts at boat launches to prevent the spread of invasive species.

Local & Regional Resources

  • Town of Stoddard, NH

    Official website for the Town of Stoddard, New Hampshire.

  • NH Fish & Game Department

    Information on wildlife, fishing regulations, and loon conservation in New Hampshire.

  • NH Division of Parks & Recreation

    State parks, boat launches, and recreational resources across New Hampshire.
